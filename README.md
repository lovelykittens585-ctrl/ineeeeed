<h2>09/29/2026 [1:40AM]</h2>
<p> added cursor, black hands of course 
in a weird way this feels like a conintuation of a video work I did over the summer. it was like a video collage of videos taken from the interent and personal audio/video I (or someone else) took. i think about what it means to turn yourself into content a lot, what does it mean to be truly vulnerable online? can i control my image? The music/text/video i added prior in the README relate to this too, I've been thinking about how Yseult directed the music video for "BITCH YOU COULD NEVER" and how another artist copied it bar for bar. ironic. i think a lot about making something of your own and it being taken away and rehashed for something else, images are like that, easily mutatible(is that a word lol?). 
<br>
it was fun playing with the capture feature, and while the text isn't an afterthought if i'm not paying attention to the text i start to pay attention to the various captures setup. i really like the push and pull between the text and the "image"/video.  </p>
